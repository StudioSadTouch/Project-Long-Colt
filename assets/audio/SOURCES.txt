Gunshots are single takes trimmed from The Free Firearm Sound Library (CC0),
created by Ben Jaszczak. Mirror:
https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary

  Standard Issue Rifle    AR-15 prepared take D_24P
  Marksman Carbine        Tikka prepared take W_24P
  Combat Shotgun          Mossberg prepared take N_30P
  Service Pistol          Walther PPQ prepared take X_31P

The energy rifle, the shotgun pump, the hit / hurt / death stingers and the
grenade placeholders are Kenney Sci-fi Sounds and Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0),
www.kenney.nl. Credit is not required.

Footsteps and the landing are Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone) by
Fantozzi (CC0), https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
  step_0..5              Fantozzi-StoneL1 R1 L2 R2 L3 R3 (alternating feet), mono,
                         trimmed to 0.19 s, 70 Hz high-pass, loudness-normalised
  land.wav               StoneL1 + StoneR2 25 ms apart, pitched to 0.85, low-passed

Menu, pause, and game-over music (ducked during a live match) is Sanctuary OS
by Dusqk, published 11 Jan 2025 by Heaven/11. Free download:
https://dusqk.bandcamp.com/album/sanctuary-os
The album is a concept mix for a console idling on its menu. Tracks play in
album order on the Music bus and then loop. Credit Dusqk if a build ships.
