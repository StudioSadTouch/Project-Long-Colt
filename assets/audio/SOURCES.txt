Weapon gunshot SFX are multi-layered acoustic studio takes and field recordings
mastered into punchy, high-energy 16-bit 44.1kHz mono WAVs (via tools/audio/generate_weapon_sounds.py).
Sources:
  1. The Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak (CC0):
     https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary
  2. Tabasco Field Gunshots by Vincent Sevedge (CC-BY 3.0):
     https://opengameart.org/content/tabasco-sounds
  3. 25 CC0 Bang / Firework SFX by rubberduck (CC0):
     https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx
  4. Kenney Sci-Fi & Impact Sounds by Kenney (CC0):
     https://kenney.nl

  standard_issue_rifle_fire.wav
    AR-15 5.56x45 near-distance hypersonic crack (D_32P) layered with SKS 7.62x39
    concussive body (sks.wav) and AK-47 bolt clatter (C_27P). 150Hz low-end thump boost,
    analog-style soft saturation, peak-limited to -0.3 dBFS, 0.420s duration.
  marksman_carbine_fire.wav
    Tikka T3 .30-06 near-distance supersonic report (W_29P) fused with Mosin Nagant
    7.62x54 near-distance punch (M_21P) and outdoor rolling acoustic decay (mosin.wav).
    130Hz resonant bass reinforcement, 0.720s duration.
  combat_shotgun_fire.wav
    Benelli Nova 12-gauge close-mic blast (O_21P) layered with Mossberg 190 (N_30P)
    and field 12ga blast (shotty.wav). 90Hz concussive pressure-wave boost (+9.7 dB RMS jump),
    0.850s room-clearing boom.
  combat_shotgun_pump.wav
    Authentic Benelli Nova 12ga full steel pump cycle (O_6) featuring slide rack-back
    and forward lock, reinforced with Tikka steel locking lug sear click (W_13). 0.550s.
  service_pistol_fire.wav
    Walther PPQ 9mm near-distance shot (X_39P) combined with CZ-52 Tokarev 7.62x25
    field take (cz.wav) for sharp mechanical slide snap and 180Hz punchy bark. 0.480s.
  der_fire.wav
    Heavy artillery cannon launch blast (cannon_04 + cannon_01) blended with deep
    rocket thruster ignition roar (thruster_fire_000) and 70Hz sub-bass launch thump. 0.950s.
  der_charged_fire.wav
    Supercharged heavy artillery detonation (cannon_02 + cannon_04) with sustained
    thruster exhaust rumble. 0.850s.
